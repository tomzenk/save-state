# Project Vision example

## Confirmed

### VIS-001 - Hierarchical community graph

Communities form a directed acyclic hierarchy. Posts can appear in ancestor communities, with relevance decreasing as graph distance increases.

User wording:

> A Dodgers post should be strongest in Dodgers, weaker in Baseball, weaker again in Sports, and only exceptionally appear in Recreation.

## Developing

### VIS-006 - Shared messaging foundation

Community chat and direct messages may share identity, notification, moderation, and storage infrastructure.

## Unrefined

### VIS-011 - Private communities

Potential invitation-only communities. Permissions, discovery, and moderation are not yet developed.
